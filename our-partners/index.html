Corporate Partner

Rosso Coffee Roasters

We're thrilled to partner with Rosso Coffee Roasters, whose generous donation of premium beans and drink vouchers is fueling more than just our festival fun, it's fueling better health. Coffee isn't just a beloved ritual; it's also been linked to meaningful liver health benefits.

Multiple studies have shown that regular coffee consumption may reduce the risk of liver disease, slow the progression of fibrosis, and even lower the chances of developing liver cancer.

That's right, your daily cup could be doing more than you think.
