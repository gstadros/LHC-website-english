Who We Are

Our Vision

To bridge liver health knowledge and the communities we serve: sharing information, supporting prevention, and encouraging care in ways that are accessible, respectful, and collaborative.

Our Mission

We work alongside communities to share liver health education in practical, relatable ways — building connections, supporting prevention, and helping people take charge of their liver health through collaboration, trust, and outreach.

Our Story

Founded in 2025 by a liver specialist with deep roots in Calgary and a strong connection to its vibrant cultural communities, Liver Health Connect was launched to improve access, spark conversation, and deliver liver health education in ways that are engaging, inclusive, and culturally meaningful.
